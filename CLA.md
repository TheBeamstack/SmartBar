### When a full CLA is *not* used

A project MAY accept contributions under **DCO sign-off only**, with no CLA,
**only if** it states in its `CONTRIBUTING.md` that it **permanently renounces
commercial dual-licensing** and will never be moved to a proprietary tier. Absent
that explicit statement, the CLA above is required (Beamstack License Framework
BLF-D3).
